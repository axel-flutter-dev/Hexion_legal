# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-01-05

At Hexicon, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (Hexicon) collects, uses, and handles your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimizing data collection. However, to provide the best experience, we collect the following types of information:

### A. Information You Provide (Optional)
If you choose to create an account or sign in to the App, we collect and store your **email address**. This is used solely to authenticate your account and allow you to restore your access or purchases across devices.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
When you use the App, we may collect certain data generated by your usage. This data is connected to an anonymous user identifier, not your personal identity (unless you are signed in). This includes:
* **Usage Data:** Information on how you interact with the app (e.g., features used, time spent).
* **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, and general region (not precise location).

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:
* To provide and maintain the App's functionality.
* To manage your account and subscriptions.
* To analyze app performance and improve stability (e.g., fixing crashes).

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
**We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.**

However, we utilize trusted third-party service providers to facilitate the App’s core functionality. These services only have access to the data necessary to perform their specific tasks and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

* **RevenueCat:** Used to manage subscriptions and validate in-app purchases.
* **Firebase / Google Analytics:** Used for backend infrastructure and to collect anonymous usage data/crash reports to improve app stability.

## 4. Data Security
We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information. We implement commercially acceptable security measures to protect your email address and usage data. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 5. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from our servers.

## 6. Your Rights (Data Deletion)
You have the right to request the deletion of your data. If you have created an account (signed in with email), you may request that your account and associated data be deleted by contacting us at the email below.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at **devlio.service@gmail.com**.
